Privacy Policy — FRXMA

Effective date: 2026-10-20

FRXMA ("the game") is made and operated by Yeoreum, an independent game developer in the Republic of Korea ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles.

1. Information the game itself collects

None. The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress (crystals, upgrades, unlocked ships, settings) is stored only on your device. On Steam, your save file may be synced by Steam Cloud, which is operated by Valve.

2. Advertising (mobile versions)

The mobile versions show optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic and interaction data. On iOS, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking when asked. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

3. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The game records only whether you own "Unlock All Ships", on your device.

4. Steam version

The Steam version uses Steamworks for achievements and cloud saves. Valve's privacy policy applies: https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/

5. Retention and your rights

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not store, share or sell any. Deleting the game removes the data stored on your device. For information processed by Google or Valve, use the links above. You can contact us at any time with questions or requests.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
